DigitalArchiveMovieJP

DigitalArchiveMovieJP is a private internal workflow operated by Verdafly to publish rights-cleared historical and cultural materials as videos on a YouTube channel managed by the operator.

Privacy Policy Terms of Use

Purpose

The workflow supports the Culture Materials Preservation activity by converting public-domain or otherwise rights-cleared materials into viewable videos and publishing them with source attribution.

Rights-first publishing

Materials are selected only when source, rights status, and publication basis can be recorded.

Internal operation

The API client is not distributed as a public app and is used only by the channel operator.

YouTube publishing

The workflow uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and set related metadata such as title, description, privacy status, and thumbnails.
