Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

DigitalArchiveMovieJP is a private internal workflow operated by Verdafly. It uses YouTube API Services to upload rights-cleared cultural archive videos to a YouTube channel managed by the operator.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the DigitalArchiveMovieJP API client and its use of YouTube API Services. The workflow is not a public application and does not provide accounts, dashboards, or services to third-party users.

2. YouTube API Services

DigitalArchiveMovieJP uses YouTube API Services. Google handles information in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. YouTube use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

3. Information Accessed, Collected, or Stored

When authorized by the channel operator, the API client may access or use YouTube account and channel permissions needed to upload videos and manage upload-related metadata. This can include authorization tokens, channel upload permissions, video titles, descriptions, privacy settings, thumbnails, YouTube video IDs, and upload results.

The workflow may also store source-material records needed for publication traceability, such as source identifiers, bibliographic metadata, rights-review status, and generated YouTube video IDs. It does not intentionally collect or store third-party YouTube user data for analytics, resale, advertising, or redistribution.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate the internal publishing workflow, verify upload results, maintain source attribution, and keep records needed for rights-cleared cultural archive publication. The API client does not sell personal data, does not use YouTube API data for advertising targeting, and does not provide YouTube API data to unauthorized third parties.

5. Sharing

Data may be transmitted to Google and YouTube when the API client uses YouTube API Services. Internal operational records may be stored in private systems controlled by the operator. Data is not sold or shared with external advertisers or data brokers.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

The public information pages under this site do not set application cookies for DigitalArchiveMovieJP. Third-party services used by the website host, browser, or linked external sites may operate under their own policies.

7. Revoking Access and Deletion Requests

The authorized Google account owner can review or revoke this API client's access from the Google security settings page. For deletion requests or privacy questions, contact admin@verdafly.com. When authorization is revoked or a deletion request is verified, stored authorization data and related internal records are deleted or de-identified where retention is not required for legal, security, or publication-record integrity reasons.

8. Security

Authorization credentials are stored in private operational environments and are not published in public repositories or public pages. Access is limited to the operator and agents acting on the operator's behalf.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the workflow, YouTube API Services usage, or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates the latest version.
